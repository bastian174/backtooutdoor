Three ways to work with Back to Outdoor – from a single content day to an ongoing content studio partnership. What fits you best, we figure out in a short conversation, not a price list.

Package 1 — Content Day

One day. Real moments. Finished content.

We join one of your tours or experiences on location and capture what actually happens – no staged production, just real guests, real reactions.

  • One shoot day right at your location
  • Edited reels + processed photos, ready for social media and your website
  • The ideal first step to see how your experiences translate into professional storytelling
→ A great way to get to know Back to Outdoor. Scope & process in the Media Kit →
Package 2 — Content Series

Individual moments become a story.

Over several weeks, a coherent content line emerges instead of isolated clips – from the first experience to the story of individual guests.

  • Several shoot days over a set period
  • A well-thought-out set of reels and photos for social media, your website, and optionally YouTube
  • A short content roadmap showing how it all fits together
→ Ideal for seasonal highlights, new experiences, or recurring tours. Scope & process in the Media Kit →
Package 3 — Content Studio Partnership

Content as a permanent part of your marketing, not a one-off project.

An ongoing collaboration with regular shoot dates and a shared editorial plan. Visible brands and gear are woven in organically where it fits – real cooperation instead of forced advertising.

  • Regular shoot dates on a shared schedule
  • A continuous stream of photo, video, and UGC content
  • Short, regular check-ins on what's working and what's next
→ For brands, hotels, and destinations that think about content long-term. Scope & process in the Media Kit →
Add-on — Guest videos

On guided tours, your guests can purchase their own personal video right on-site – extra value for them, an extra revenue stream for you. Or gift it to your guests as a keepsake. Runs alongside any of the three packages.

Frequently asked questions
Why isn't there any pricing here?

Because every project is different – the size of your tours, how often you need content, whether it's a one-off experience or an ongoing partnership. After a short conversation, you'll get an offer tailored exactly to your plans.

Can we start small?

Yes. The Content Day is exactly for that – a manageable first step, with no long-term commitment required.

How long until we have finished content?

With the Content Day: usually a few days after the shoot. For the Content Series and Partnership, we agree on the rhythm together.
