The Most Beautiful Villages & Towns on the French Riviera – Hidden Gems & Highlights

2025-03-03

The Côte d'Azur is known for its luxurious seaside resorts, dreamy beaches and azure-blue sea. But beyond the touristy hotspots, there are numerous charming villages and historic towns brimming with unique character, history and breathtaking views. Here are some hidden gems and highlights you shouldn't miss on your trip!

1. Èze – The Medieval Gem With a Sea View

  • Highlights: Narrow lanes, exotic garden, spectacular sea views

  • Worth seeing: Jardin Exotique, castle ruins, Fragonard perfume factory

  • Why visit? Perfect for romantics and photography lovers

The charming hilltop village of Èze perches high above the coast and offers one of the most beautiful panoramic views in the whole region. The winding lanes, small boutiques and the exotic garden make this a must-visit spot.

2. Saint-Paul-de-Vence – Art & Culture in a Picturesque Village

  • Highlights: Artists' village, galleries, medieval town walls

  • Worth seeing: Fondation Maeght, town walls, small craft shops

  • Why visit? Ideal for art lovers and culture enthusiasts

Saint-Paul-de-Vence is one of the best-known artists' villages in France and has inspired famous painters like Picasso and Chagall. The picturesque old town and the many small galleries make this a cultural highlight.

3. Menton – The Town of Lemons and Mediterranean Colours

  • Highlights: Colourful facades, lemon festival, botanical gardens

  • Worth seeing: Old town, Serre de la Madone, Jardin du Palais Carnolès lemon garden

  • Why visit? Mediterranean flair and culinary delights

Menton is famous for its lemon festival and its pleasant, almost Italian atmosphere. The colourful houses, charming old town and dreamy gardens offer a relaxed alternative to the better-known spots on the French Riviera.

4. Villefranche-sur-Mer – The Picturesque Harbour Town

  • Highlights: Fishing harbour, colourful facades, sandy town beach

  • Worth seeing: Saint-Elme Citadel, old town lanes, harbour promenade

  • Why visit? Perfect for a relaxed day trip

Villefranche-sur-Mer impresses with its authentic old town and deep blue water. The small cafés by the harbour invite you to linger, while the sandy beach is a rare exception along this otherwise rocky coastline.

5. Grasse – France's Perfume Capital

  • Highlights: Perfume factories, fragrance museums, flower fields

  • Worth seeing: Fragonard perfume house, old town, Notre-Dame-du-Puy Cathedral

  • Why visit? A paradise for fragrance lovers and culture fans

Grasse is considered the world capital of perfume and impresses with its historic perfume factories. Here you can join a scent tour and learn more about the centuries-old tradition of perfume making.

Conclusion: Must-See Destinations Away From the Crowds

The Côte d'Azur isn't just glamorous seaside resorts – it's also home to numerous charming villages and historic towns with a unique character. Whether you're looking for art, history, Mediterranean atmosphere or dreamy views, you'll find the perfect destination here!

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

  1. What is the most beautiful village on the Côte d'Azur?
    Èze is considered one of the most beautiful villages, with a spectacular sea view.

  2. Which town on the Côte d'Azur is a hidden gem?
    Menton is a colourful town that's often less crowded than Nice or Cannes.

  3. Is Grasse worth a visit?
    Yes! Grasse is the world capital of perfume and offers fascinating insights into the art of fragrance.

  4. Are there any authentic fishing villages left on the Côte d'Azur?
    Villefranche-sur-Mer is a picturesque harbour town with traditional charm.

  5. Which villages on the Côte d'Azur are good for day trips?
    Saint-Paul-de-Vence, Èze and Villefranche-sur-Mer are perfect for a day trip from Nice or Cannes.
